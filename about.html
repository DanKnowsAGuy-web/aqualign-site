The story

The science behind AquAlign began with an industrial experiment. A research team proposed testing an experimental water treatment in fabric dyeing, a process that consumes enormous quantities of water. The treated process used less water, less dye, less time, and less energy, and both the quality-control testers and the models remarked that the treated cloth felt softer.

Janice Byerlay, then the chief operations officer of the company running that dye house, had a science background and recognized what she was looking at. She joined the research effort, spent years testing applications personally, and when lab results became consistently replicable, the team asked her to lead the push to commercialization.

She recruited business partners: the consultant, author, and speaker Ian Percy, and entrepreneur and engineer Cliff Suljak, with whom she turned the science into a working technology, its first iteration focused on water, back where the research began. Entrepreneur Brian Marshall provided the investment and commercial experience that carried it from technology to product, through commercial field trials and into customers' hands.

Between 2012 and 2013, that company ran the documented field trials you can read on the Results page: corn, greenhouse tomatoes, celery, tobacco, alfalfa, kenaf, and a 42,000-hen commercial egg demonstration. The research lineage runs back further still: the team's work with fisheries operations began in 1996.

In 2026, the original team relaunched the company as AquAlign. Same technology, same evidence discipline, a new generation of systems, and a new demonstration program opening with the 2026–27 season.

What we believe

  • Sustainability and the bottom line aren't opposites. Water that performs better means fewer inputs for equal or better results.
  • Simple is better. No power, no chemicals, no complexity, on your farm or in our own operations.
  • Say what it won't do. Trust is built in the disclaimers, not despite them. AquAlign conditions water; it does not clean it, and we put that sentence in our footer.
  • Made in North America, serviced by name. Independent representatives who know their customers bring us to you, follow every install, and stay in touch.

Stewardship in practice: the 3R program

Every AquAlign cartridge is designed for Reduce, Reuse, Recycle. Spent cartridges return to us for component re-purposing and recycling, a closed loop that keeps cartridges out of landfills and credits you 35% of the replacement cost for participating. Over 98% of the cartridge, and 85% of the full system, meets the 3R criteria.
