+31%

Corn yield, non-irrigated, in a drought year

Smith Farms · Utopia, Ontario · 2012 · single treatment at planting

Read the trialClose

One of the hottest, driest growing seasons in a decade. Treated and untreated non-GMO seed planted with the same seed drill in the same unirrigated field: fertilized once, herbicide once, then left to the weather. AquAlign was applied exactly once, at planting.

By day 14, treated plants averaged 12.4 inches against 7.3 for control, with roughly 50% thicker stalks and visibly stronger roots. By late July: 7.1 feet versus 5.6. At harvest, the treated rows delivered more, larger, heavier, kernel-dense cobs: a net yield 31% above control.

"In one of the hottest, driest years I can recall, from sprout through to harvest, the AquAlign results were significantly and measurably better."

Tom Smith, Smith Farms; named one of the 100 Most Influential Canadians in Agriculture of the 20th century

Afterward: Smith Farms extended AquAlign across all its corn fields and soybean plantings the following season.

83days

Longer greenhouse tomato production, plus 15.3% higher brix

Van Hart Greenhouses · Holland Marsh, Ontario · 2012

Read the trialClose

Certified organic since the 1980s, Van Hart Greenhouses runs a high-intensity computerized nutrient irrigation system that produces some of the highest per-plant yields recorded, at the cost of exhausting plants by mid-season. One greenhouse's irrigation manifold received AquAlign; a second house with the same varietal served as control.

The control planting was exhausted and removed on schedule, July 16, after 38 days of production. The AquAlign house kept producing fruit and flowers until the second hard frost forced removal on October 8: 121 days. One treated planting out-produced two control plantings. Brix averaged 7.68 versus 6.5, sweeter and denser fruit.

Afterward: Van Hart extended AquAlign to all greenhouses the next season and diverted conditioned water to field crops with similar gains.

Water consumption by a 42,000-hen flock, with production decline slowed 27%

Canada's largest vertically integrated egg producer · 2013 · 8-week controlled demonstration

Read the trialClose

An 8-week controlled demonstration on a flock of 42,000 ISA Browns, comparing an 8-week control period (flock weeks 44 to 51) against the demonstration period (weeks 56 to 63). A mechanical failure unrelated to the system caused a partial-flock dehydration incident mid-trial; the results below include it.

  • Water consumption down 259 gallons per day: 14,504 gallons (11%) over the demonstration period, on better-hydrated birds.
  • Rate of age-related production decline reduced by 27%.
  • Average egg weight of 62.2 g through the demo; ideal weight criteria held to the end of the flock's cycle.
  • Fewer deformed and floating eggs.
  • Manure significantly drier with sharply reduced free ammonia: easier to store, sellable at a premium.

"We sell 90,000 tons of manure a year and at even an additional dollar per ton, that more than pays for the AquAlign systems, before the benefits delivered to our primary egg revenue."

Operations manager

98vs 80%

Alfalfa germination, and zero die-off after 120 hours without water

Controlled demonstration · 2012 · water only, no nutrients

Read the trialClose

Two identical trays, certified-organic seed from a single lot, identical dual-filtered municipal water: one tray's water conditioned, one not. Ten days of growth, then a five-day total water cutoff to simulate drought stress.

Day 10: 98% germination versus 80%, average height 44 mm versus 20 mm. Market-ready sprouts against barely-started controls. After 120 hours dry, control suffered 25% die-off and roughly 39% die-back; conditioned plants had zero die-off with only minor leaf fade. When watering resumed, conditioned plants recovered fully within two days; control die-off passed 50%.

A companion year-long field demonstration ran under center-pivot irrigation at K-C Farms, Colorado, where the owner recorded brix readings of 23 against a control of 7: "I have never seen a brix reading over 15 here."

+40%

Celery germination in the optimal-condition comparison (11% overall)

Horling Farms · 2012–13 · standard seed, automated line

Read the trialClose

Celery is notoriously hard to germinate commercially. One skid (180 flats of 338 cells) was seeded through the automated line with conditioned water; the control skid ran the next day on untreated water. A complication worked against the trial: the treated flats' greenhouse averaged 6°F hotter than the control house, above celery's optimal range.

Despite the temperature handicap, treated flats germinated 11% better overall. Comparing the two flats closest to optimal temperature: 40% better. The grower's takeaway was a floor of 11% improvement using standard seed instead of premium germination-enhanced stock, with more expected under normal conditions.

14days early

Tobacco to harvest ahead of frost, +30% yield, Choice Quality grade

Dirse Farms · Walsingham, Ontario · 2012 · seedling water only

Read the trialClose

Conditioned water was used only in the greenhouse irrigation of starter seedlings, nothing in the field. The goal: faster maturity, after frost losses in prior years.

Treated plants reached harvest a full 14 days ahead of control. An early frost then hit the control field with only 25% harvested, exactly the risk the grower was trying to beat. Against that harvested portion, treated plants yielded 30% more, and graded Choice Quality, tobacco's top tier. Tobacco shares its family with tomato, pepper, and potato; the trial was designed to read across.

"Bringing the crop in 2 weeks before the frost hit, with a grading of Choice Quality, made all the difference to our year's bottom-line."

Dirse Farms

How it works. The honest version.

Water passes through a housing containing our proprietary crystalline cartridge media. Our research team's approach is based on epitaxy: the transfer of structural information from a solid surface to a liquid in contact with it, a phenomenon studied in materials science for decades and used routinely in semiconductor manufacturing.

Chemically, nothing is added and nothing changes. What changes is measured performance: germination counts, growth measurements, yield weights, brix readings, water-meter readings. We know how water conditioning sounds; the category has earned its skepticism. That's why we don't ask you to believe a mechanism. We publish dated trials with their methods, and we state plainly what the system won't do.
