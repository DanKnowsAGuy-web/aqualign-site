The system · the science · the sizing

How it works. The honest version.

No chemicals, no additives, no power, no moving parts. Here is what the system is, what our research says it does, what we claim, and what we refuse to claim.

What the system is

A rugged in-line housing containing a replaceable cartridge. Water flows through a designed pattern that maximizes contact with the cartridge media, then continues into your irrigation or drinking lines. There is no power hookup, no dosing equipment, and nothing to monitor. Annual cartridge replacement, plus periodic housing cleaning as your flow rates indicate, is the entire maintenance schedule.

What happens to the water

Chemically, nothing. No additives, no ion exchange, no filtration. Our research approach is built on epitaxy: the transfer of structural information from the surface of a crystalline solid to a liquid in contact with it, a phenomenon studied in materials science for decades and used routinely in semiconductor manufacturing. The cartridge media is a proprietary crystalline material engineered to condition the water's molecular organization as it passes.

"Epitaxy is the transmission of structural information from the surface (hence epi) of one material (usually a crystalline solid) to another (usually but not always a liquid)… without any material transfer."

Roy, Tiller, Bell & Hoover, "The Structure of Liquid Water," 2005 (the paper the original research team worked from)

What we claim, and what we don't

We understand the skepticism around water conditioning, and we don't ask you to take the mechanism on faith. Our claims rest on what can be measured in a field, a greenhouse, or a barn: germination counts, growth measurements, yield weights, brix readings, water-meter readings. Those trials, methods, complications, and all, are published in full on the Results page.

Every documented trial is dated and attributed. In field and commercial trials, improvements on the primary measure ranged from 11% to 31%, with larger gains in controlled studies; our working expectation for a new installation is 10%+ when major uncontrolled variables cooperate. We would rather under-promise and show you the data.

Sizing your system

One conversation with our team covers it:

  • Your application (crop, livestock, hydroculture, industrial)
  • Pipe size and type
  • Peak maximum flow rate, in gallons per minute
  • System pressure rating, in PSI
  • Any special considerations in how your operation uses water

Maintenance and the 3R program

Clean the housing as your flow rates indicate. Replace the cartridge once a year: under the 3R program it ships to you automatically, and returning the spent cartridge in the same box earns a 35% credit. Returned cartridges are disassembled; components are sterilized and re-purposed or recycled. Over 98% of the cartridge is recyclable material, and none of it goes to landfill.

What it won't do

AquAlign does not filter, purify, soften, or remediate water, and cannot make contaminated water safe. If your water has toxins, chemical contamination, or a history of either, talk to our technical staff before installing anything.
