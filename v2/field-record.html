What science says about water at surfaces

For the technical reader: the science of water at surfaces

Mainstream Water next to surfaces is more ordered.

Water within a few molecular layers of a surface, and inside living cells, behaves differently from bulk water. Its role in biology is an active research area.

Chaplin, Nat Rev Mol Cell Biol 2006; Ball, PNAS 2017

Reproduced across labs Exclusion zones.

Several labs have reproduced Gerald Pollack's finding that water next to some water-loving surfaces forms a distinct, ordered zone that pushes particles away. Research into exactly why continues.

Elton et al., Int J Mol Sci 2020, 21(14):5041

Peer-reviewed animal studies Animals drinking structured water.

A 2021 peer-reviewed review reports consistent results across several studies: faster growth, lower oxidative-stress markers and higher tissue conductivity, a marker of hydration. A 2020 trial in Thoroughbred racehorses (17 treated, 15 controls, 4 weeks) reported better hydration by bioelectrical impedance. These studies looked at other structured-water products.

Lindinger, J Anim Sci 2021, 99(5):skab063 · Lindinger & Northrop, Vet Sci Res 2020
